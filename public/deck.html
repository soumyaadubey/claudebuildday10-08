Hi, we built TrialFinder. It helps patients find clinical trials near them that they may be a good fit for, explains each one in plain language, and lets them listen in their own language. Keep this to one sentence and move on.

Maria is who we designed for. Her depression hasn't gotten better after two medicines. There may be research studies a few subway stops away that are looking for exactly her, but nobody has told her, and she isn't a medical expert. She's also more comfortable in Spanish.

The problem

Trials exist. Patients like Maria rarely hear about them, and the listings aren't written for them.

1 Hard to find

No one points her to studies. Searching registries means knowing the right medical terms first.

2 Written for researchers

Exclusion Criteria: * Personal or close family history of schizophrenia or bipolar I disorder * Inadequate response to ≥2 antidepressants at adequate dose and duration…

3 English only

Listings, summaries and next steps assume she reads medical English comfortably.

How it works

Four steps, from "I don't know where to start" to "someone will call me."

1

Tell us

Condition, age, ZIP, how far you can travel, and your health in your own words.

2

We search

Live ClinicalTrials.gov results: recruiting studies within your distance.

3

We explain

Claude reads each study's criteria and writes a plain summary, why it may or may not fit, and questions to ask.

4

You decide

Listen in 7 languages. Contact a study only if you choose, with consent.

Before any of this: a safety check reads what you wrote. If you might be in crisis, we stop and connect you to 988.

Safety by design

Built for someone who may be struggling.

You don't have to go through this alone.

Call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, any time.

Call 988 If you are in immediate danger, call 911.
  • Crisis check runs firstIf someone writes about ending their life, we show no trials, only support.
  • Never says "you're eligible"Only the study team decides. We never diagnose.
  • Never invents factsIf the listing doesn't say it, it goes under "things the study team would check."
  • Always visible"TrialFinder is software, not a doctor" on every screen.
TrialFinder7
Our users may be struggling, so safety comes first. Before any search, Claude classifies what they wrote. If it signals a crisis, we stop and show this calm screen with 988, and 911 for immediate danger. No trials are shown. And in the matching, Claude is told never to say someone is eligible, never to diagnose, and never to invent facts.

What's next

What it takes to make this real.

Real study teams

Route requests to actual site coordinators instead of a demo queue.

Clinician review

Have clinicians and patients evaluate summaries and fit reasons for accuracy and tone.

Privacy and compliance

Secure storage, accounts and HIPAA review before any real patient data.

Beyond NYC

Real geocoding instead of a bundled ZIP table, and accurate site addresses.

Test with real users

Usability sessions with screen-reader users and people with low health literacy.

More languages

Translate the whole interface, not just the summaries, and add more languages.
