How it works

From "I don't know where to start" to "someone will call me."

1

Tell us about you

Your condition, age, ZIP code and how far you can travel, plus anything you'd like to share in your own words.

2

We find and explain

We search ClinicalTrials.gov for studies recruiting near you, then explain each one simply: what it involves and why it may or may not fit.

3

You decide

Listen in your language, write down questions to ask, and contact a study team only if you want to.

Our promises

Built carefully, for people who deserve care.

Your safety comes first

If what you write suggests you may be in crisis, we pause the search and connect you with the 988 Lifeline.

We never decide for you

TrialFinder doesn't diagnose or decide eligibility. Only a study's team can say whether a study is right for you.

Honest about what we don't know

If a study listing doesn't say something, we tell you, and list it as a question for the study team.

A person reviews every request

Nothing is shared without your consent, and a study coordinator reviews each request before reaching out.
